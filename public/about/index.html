About ThaiType

Helping everyone type Thai with confidence.

ThaiType is a focused learning platform for people who want to type Thai accurately, naturally, and with confidence. We start with the Kedmanee keyboard and turn every keystroke into useful practice.

Our promise: ThaiType's core typing practice will remain free for everyone.

Why we exist

Thai typing deserves a better way to learn.

Learning Thai does not stop at reading and speaking. For students, teachers, professionals, and anyone living or working in Thailand, typing is a practical skill used every day.

Yet most learners are left to memorize a keyboard layout on their own. ThaiType makes that process clear and measurable with focused exercises, a visual Kedmanee keyboard, instant feedback, and meaningful progress data.

We are building ThaiType as a product family: a free practice tool for everyone, an advanced personal experience for committed learners, and a separate platform for teachers and organizations.

How we build

Simple practice. Useful results.

Every part of ThaiType follows three principles.

Keep practice accessible

The essential tools for learning the Thai keyboard stay free and easy to start.

Measure real progress

Speed matters, but accuracy, weak keys, consistency, and improvement matter too.

Respect each audience

Free learners, individual members, and organizations get separate, focused experiences.

One mission, three products

Choose the ThaiType experience that fits you.

Each product has its own purpose and website, so free practice remains focused while personal and organizational tools can grow independently.
