Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

ThaiType (“we”, “our”) is a Thai typing practice web app. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your basic profile (name, email, avatar) from that provider.
  • Practice data — Typing results such as WPM, accuracy, mode, and test duration, used for personal history and the global leaderboard.
  • Guest use — You may practice without signing in. Guest sessions are not saved to our database or ranked.

2. How we use information

  • To provide and improve the typing practice experience
  • To show personal bests, recent scores, and global rankings
  • To keep your account secure and the service reliable

3. Storage & third parties

Account and score data are stored using Supabase. Sign-in is handled by Google and/or Facebook OAuth. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies & local data

We use browser storage and session cookies for authentication and basic app function. We also use Google Analytics to understand product usage and Google AdSense to support the free service with advertising. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

5. Your choices

  • Continue as a guest without creating an account
  • Sign out at any time from the app header
  • Request deletion of your account data by contacting us

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the site operator via the project repository or the email associated with this service.

By using ThaiType, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
